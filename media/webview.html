BobGraph panels

Every region of the visualizer, and what it does.

Controls
Left panel. Refreshes the graph, exports it as JSON, and holds the Font Size preference. Drag its right edge to resize it.
Graph
Centre panel. One node per file, edges show imports. Click a node for its details, double-click to open that file's flowchart. Use the +/−/▟ buttons to zoom.
Node Details
Top of the right panel. Shows the selected node's description, connections and AI-written pseudocode, plus an Open in IDE button that jumps to the source line. Drag the divider below it to resize.
AI Assistant
Bottom of the right panel. Ask questions about the selected node. Needs a language model, so it stays empty until one is available.
Panel toggles
Top-right of the graph. ? opens this help, ◀ and ▶ collapse or restore each side panel. Your layout is remembered.
